About this scenario

The 2026, 2050 and 2070 maps are interpretive snapshots of a scenario pathway, not forecasts or quantitative measures of bilateral ties. 2026 introduces its starting conditions; 2050 and 2070 imagine how relationships could evolve. The scenario is adapted from the supplied “East Asia — Three scenarios” draft. KorMem Alliance succeeds Samsung and SK Hynix in this fictional future.

The Q-Garden, living-compute cartridges and everyday encounters are fictional details. The artwork is AI-generated. This is not a Samsung publication.

Geography: Natural Earth. Interaction reference: BCG’s Beyond Tomorrow.
