About this scenario

Baeksang imagines a 2070 world where East Asian stories, songs and games attract young creators from across the world. Seoul is a leading festival, but Tokyo, Bangkok, Jakarta, Mumbai and Lagos all shape a contested cultural circuit. The simplified system diagram is adapted from the supplied relationship chart. The 2026 and 2050 views are interpretive steps toward the supplied 2070 narrative, not forecasts or quantitative measures of ties.

The six festival places adapt the supplied one-page and extended Baeksang stories. People, institutions and events in 2070 are fictional. The Seoul artwork is AI-generated.
