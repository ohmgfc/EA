Futures of East Asia — Scenario 03: Baeksang

Open index.html in a modern browser after extracting the complete ZIP. Keep the
HTML, JavaScript, CSS and assets folder together. No installation, account,
server or internet connection is needed for the interactive experience.

Explore the 2026, 2050 and 2070 interpretive snapshots with the timeline.
Select an actor in the simplified system diagram to read its role and relationships. In 2070, select
South Korea once for its overview and again to enter the Baeksang festival.
Inside the festival, select any of six places or use the list at right. The
"Trace the international relationship" action returns to the related actor.

The 2070 content adapts the two supplied Baeksang scenario documents. The
earlier diagram stages are interpretive steps toward that fictional world and are
not forecasts or quantitative measures. The city image is AI-generated.
The system diagram adapts the supplied relationship chart and simplifies its
actors and links for readability.

This is a standalone Scenario 03 adaptation of the supplied Silicon Shield
offline webpage. The original Scenario 02 files were not altered.
