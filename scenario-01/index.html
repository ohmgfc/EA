This scenario explores how care could reorganise cities. The network shows city roles and dependencies, not geographical positions, measured flows or exclusive city categories. A city may fulfil several roles.

2026 sets out the starting pressures; 2050 and 2070 are fictional scenario snapshots, not forecasts. The future compact and mobility arrangements belong to this scenario and do not describe current law. National governments retain immigration and security powers.

The building is an illustrative section of one possible care city. Its service levels make different functions visible; the city types are roles, not named places. The illustration is AI-generated. Interaction reference: BCG’s Beyond Tomorrow.
