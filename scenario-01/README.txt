SILVER CITY COMPACT — SCENARIO 1

Open index.html in a modern browser. Keep the assets folder alongside it. This experience works offline.

Drag between 2026, 2050 and 2070 to explore relationships between city types. In 2070, select Care cities once for information, then again to enter the care building.

The service levels run from top to bottom: service committee; health and social centre with older people’s residences; rehabilitation; digital sustainability; emergency care at street level; mortuary and transfer holding in the basement. Select a level to explore its role and trace its city relationships.

Keyboard: Tab between controls; Enter or Space to select; arrow keys to change the timeline; Escape to close details or return to the network. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

This is a fictional future, expressed in British English. City types are general roles. The illustration is AI-generated. The optional Scenario 2 and reference links require internet access.

The digital sustainability ward combines virtual reality headsets with fictional neurolinks for embodied virtual experiences.
